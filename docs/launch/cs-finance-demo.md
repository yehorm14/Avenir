## Three-minute walkthrough

1. **0:00 — Choose a field.** Open Home and select Computer Science. Explain the decision: compare career evidence, choose a training route, take one practical step.
2. **0:20 — Understand the evidence.** Software developer is the representative occupation. Distinguish the workforce median from the National Careers Service starting-pay guide. Switch Real pay → Nominal → Jobs; explain inflation adjustment and the 2021 occupation-code break.
3. **0:55 — Compare related work.** Switch to Data analyst, then back to Software developer. Each occupation has its own observations and source dates. Automation exposure is a research estimate of tasks, not a probability of job loss.
4. **1:20 — Explore Finance.** Open Finance from the top navigation. Compare analyst and accountant roles. Keep the published sector scenario's original horizon and geography; it is not a personal earnings forecast.
5. **1:50 — Choose a next action.** Show the finance starter exercise, course examples and learning/work-experience links. Save a relevant link. These are discovery links, not claims of a current opening.
6. **2:15 — Build a plan.** Choose Build my plan. Compare university and apprenticeship routes, then open My plan to add a personal reason, choose a next action or export the plan. Refresh to demonstrate persistence.
7. **2:45 — Show the basis.** Return to a subject dashboard and open Sources and coverage. Explain the separation between sourced observations, deterministic analysis and prepared career guidance. The dashboard works with supplied snapshots and needs no live model call.
