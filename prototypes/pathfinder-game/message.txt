GUTS ideas, re-scored with unlimited time and resources
Sat 17 Oct 2026. What changed: time, money, API keys, data licences and labelled training data are no longer limits. The Aladdin brief and the judges stay the same. This is the summary version; the 6-hour analysis is in guts-idea-comparison-and-final-plan.md.
The answer in four lines
The ideas bunch up. Remove the constraints and all six land between 26 and 31 out of 40. Feasibility was doing most of the separating.
Late gains the most. Every blocker that sank it tonight is something money buys: time, the WRDS licence, the data pulls, a careful backtest. What money can't buy is its weak spot: whether the edge still exists, regulatory exposure, and how hard it is to explain.
The career ideas stay safe but cap out. Their structural risk is low, but originality and proof are limited, because careers advice can only be checked years later.
Best overall: a bigger career hybrid, "Pathfinder Full". It borrows Late's strongest idea at full scale: a multi-year backtest that grades its own signal. Late is the better bet only if its headline statistic holds on 2022+ data (section 5).

4. Unconstrained recommendation: Pathfinder Full
A school leaver gets the analysis an investor would expect before committing years to a career: fit, pay, demand, AI exposure, confidence, a hedge career and a next step. Unlike every other careers tool, it publishes its own track record.
Layer
What it adds over the 6-hour plan
From
Discovery
Licensed practitioner clips lead to activities, which lead to occupations. A fine-tuned interest matcher, tested against keyword search on held-out examples
B
Data
Commercial job-ad data coded to SOC 2020 (e.g. Lightcast) for regional, weekly demand, alongside the official sources (Occupations in Demand, ASHE). Apprenticeship feeds for all four nations. AI exposure at task level

A multi-year backtest. For each past year, rebuild the demand and exposure signal from data available then, and check employment and pay 3 to 5 years later. A "pick a past year" button replays it, like Late's random-day button

D, C
Why this and not Late, even with unlimited resources:
It keeps the lowest structural risk.
The backtest removes the strongest argument in Late's own pitch, that "a career recommendation can't be checked".
It stays on-theme for BlackRock through the investment framing.
Its honest weak points:
The backtest crosses the 2021 change in job codes (SOC 2010 to SOC 2020), so the year-to-year bridge needs checking.
Occupation-years are few and confounded, so the track record will be indicative, not proof.
5. When Late is the better call
Late's ceiling is real. Compute its headline statistic first, then choose Late if all three hold:
The effect survives out of sample. On 2022+ data, the stale or recombined-story reversal and the "you're late" verdict beat chance after costs. Supplier lag alone is not enough: it faded after publication.
It stays a learning tool. The team frames it the way the brief's "market learning" row does: help a learner choose what to investigate, never what to buy or sell.
The licences allow it. The data licences cover showing results to a sponsor and in a public repo.
Otherwise, build Pathfinder Full and keep Late as the sister product. Both share the same core: evidence first, every number sourced, and a published self-grade.
